Workforce Analytics

Attendance & schedule compliance · Power BI report · Presented by Olga Sasinovich

Open the report in a new tab Read the case study

The report runs on synthetic data: employees, departments, clock-in / clock-out events and leave records are generated for the portfolio. Use the date and month slicers and the buttons inside the report to switch the view, and the button on Daily to open the “Why is the hours balance off?” drill-down page. For the best view, open the report in a new tab or use the full-screen button at the bottom right of the report.
